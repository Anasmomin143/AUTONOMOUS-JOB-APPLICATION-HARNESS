## Voice notes for outbound messages

The user's LinkedIn voice is:

- direct, unornamented, sentence-fragment style;
- shows the work by naming products (Capture, Travel Edge, IndiGo),
  never by listing generic buzzwords;

tone. Anything that reads like a template ("I am writing to express my
strong interest…") gets rejected by the resume/message validator.
